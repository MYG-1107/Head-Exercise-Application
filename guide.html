Quick guide

Use the movement counter

The counter opens directly. No account or login is required. Choose camera-assisted estimates or camera-free manual counting.

1. Choose a mode

Choose Start camera to request camera permission, or select Use manual mode to count without a camera.

2. Calibrate camera mode

Keep your face visible and hold a comfortable, forward-facing position while the calibration indicator advances. Calibration is only a reference for relative direction estimates; it is not a posture assessment.

3. Count a complete movement

One repetition is a direction followed by a return to centre. Changing directly from one direction to another does not count a repetition. Manual entries are self-reported.

4. Pause, stop or export

Use Pause for a break and Stop when finished. Counts stay in the current page session until reset, refresh or close. Select Export summary to download a CSV file to your device.

Camera tracking is an estimate and may be affected by lighting, framing or interruptions. Read the safety information before using the application and review the privacy details to understand camera and session-data handling.
