Quick start

How to use Head Exercise Application

The counter opens directly. No account or login is required. Choose camera-assisted estimates or camera-free manual counting.

1. Choose a mode

Camera mode estimates relative direction from facial landmarks. Manual mode does not request camera access and lets you record a completed movement cycle yourself. Choose the mode that is practical for your device and situation.

2. Start camera mode only when ready

Press Start camera to trigger the browser's permission prompt. If you grant permission, the preview appears and the tracking model loads. You can deny permission and continue with manual mode.

3. Allow neutral calibration

Keep your face visible and hold a comfortable forward-facing position while the calibration indicator advances. Calibration creates a reference for relative direction estimates; it is not a posture assessment.

4. Understand one repetition

A completed repetition requires a direction followed by a return to centre. A direct change from one direction to another does not complete that cycle. Camera estimates can be interrupted by lighting, framing or tracking quality.

5. Pause, stop and export

Use Pause when taking a break and Stop when finishing. The counts remain in the page session until reset, refresh or close. Choose Export summary to download a CSV copy to your device.
