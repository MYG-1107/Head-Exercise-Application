Safety

Intended use and limitations

Head Exercise Application is a movement-counting utility. It is not a medical device, clinical assessment, diagnosis or treatment plan.

What the application records

The application counts direction-to-centre cycles using camera-assisted estimates or manual entries. It does not recommend which movements are appropriate, assess posture, measure clinical range of motion or confirm exercise technique.

Camera estimates have limits

Camera mode estimates relative direction from facial landmarks. Lighting, camera position, face visibility, device performance and tracking interruptions can affect its output. It may miss or misclassify movement. Manual entries are self-reported and are not independently verified by the application.

Use comfortable movement

Do not force a movement or continue to reach a displayed goal. Stop if an activity causes pain, dizziness, numbness, weakness or other concerning symptoms. Ask a qualified healthcare professional about safe and appropriate movement when you have health questions.

Do not rely on counts as medical evidence

A displayed total records interactions with the application. It is not a diagnosis, treatment outcome, validated measurement or substitute for professional assessment and care.
