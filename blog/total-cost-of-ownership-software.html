The Total Cost of Ownership of Custom Software

The initial build estimate is only one part of a software investment. A realistic business case includes the people, services and operating work required after launch.

8 November 2026·General educational information

Look beyond the initial build

A project may require discovery, design, development, testing, migration, deployment, training and documentation. Later costs can include hosting, domains, API usage, monitoring, backups, security updates, support and improvements. Compare deliverables and responsibilities, not only headline price.

Model costs over a useful horizon

Estimate one-time and recurring costs separately. Identify usage-based charges and internal effort for product decisions, reviews and vendor management. Use low, expected and high scenarios, and label which numbers are estimates or contractually fixed.

Connect spending to business value

Expected benefits might include less manual work, better consistency or new service capability. Define how each benefit will be observed. Review actual operating costs and outcomes after launch; an unsupported savings estimate should not be presented as guaranteed return.

Key points

  • Include operations and internal effort in estimates.
  • Use scenarios and label assumptions clearly.
  • Measure realised benefits after launch.
