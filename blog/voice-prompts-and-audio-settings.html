Using Voice Prompts and Audio Settings

Voice prompts are an optional convenience. They are off by default and can be enabled or disabled with the voice control in the tracker interface.

8 November 2026·Application help guide

Voice is optional

The voice control changes whether the application uses the browser's speech synthesis feature for prompts. It does not affect the camera model, movement estimate or counting rules. The control displays its current state so you can understand whether audio feedback is enabled.

Why prompts may not sound

Speech synthesis support, available voices, browser settings, device volume and operating-system audio controls vary. Some browsers also restrict speech until the user interacts with the page. If audio does not work, use the visible direction and status text.

Turn voice off at any time

Select the voice control again to switch prompts off. The app cancels queued speech when voice is disabled. You can keep using the counter with audio off; the interface does not require spoken instructions.

Consider your environment

Use audio only when it is helpful and appropriate. Headphones or speaker volume are controlled by your device, not by the application. The app does not record microphone audio and does not need microphone permission for voice prompts.

Key points

  • Voice prompts start off by default.
  • The browser provides the speech feature.
  • Visible text remains available when audio is off.
  • The application does not record microphone audio.
Use and safety note

This guide explains application features, not health treatment or exercise technique. The application is a counting utility, not a medical device or clinical assessment tool. Use only comfortable movement and seek qualified professional advice for health questions.
