Cloud Cost Management: FinOps Basics for Product Owners

Cloud bills can grow quietly as services, environments and traffic expand. Cost management works best when engineering and finance share visibility and responsibility.

8 November 2026·General educational information

Make spend visible

Group cloud costs by product, environment, team or workload using tags where supported. Identify major recurring cost drivers and look for idle resources, oversized instances, unused storage and unexpected data transfer. Compare cost with actual workload activity.

Set budgets and ownership

Assign owners to major services and define who receives alerts. Establish separate budgets for production, testing and experimentation. Forecasts should state assumptions about traffic, storage, retention and third-party pricing, and use scenarios where demand is uncertain.

Optimise without harming reliability

Evaluate caching, storage lifecycle rules and service tiers alongside their impact on availability, recovery and security. Review spend regularly with finance and technical owners, then confirm that savings occurred without degrading the product.

Key points

  • Tag and assign ownership to major cloud costs.
  • Model spend against workload and usage.
  • Balance savings with security and reliability.
