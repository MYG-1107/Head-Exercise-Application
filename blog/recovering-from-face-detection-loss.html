What to Do When Face Detection Is Lost

Camera mode needs a usable face view to estimate direction. When tracking loses the face, the application interrupts the current movement rather than completing an unfinished count.

8 November 2026·Application help guide

Read the status message

The live status may show that no face is detected. Move back into the camera frame and check the preview. Avoid assuming that the last displayed direction remains valid while the face is not being tracked.

Why an unfinished cycle is interrupted

A completed repetition requires a direction and a return to centre. When the face disappears during a movement, the app interrupts that movement state. This reduces the chance of counting a cycle that the tracker did not observe from start to finish.

Restore a stable view

Check the device position, light and face framing. Wait for the model to detect the face again. If the neutral reference no longer seems reliable after a major change, use Recalibrate while the session is running.

Switch modes when needed

Repeated tracking loss can make camera mode frustrating. Stop the camera session and choose manual mode if it is more practical. The manual counter records your entries but does not verify the movement.

Key points

  • No-face status means the tracker cannot currently estimate direction.
  • An interrupted cycle should not be counted as complete.
  • Restore the face view and recalibrate if the baseline changed.
  • Manual mode is available when camera tracking remains unreliable.
Use and safety note

This guide explains application features, not health treatment or exercise technique. The application is a counting utility, not a medical device or clinical assessment tool. Use only comfortable movement and seek qualified professional advice for health questions.
