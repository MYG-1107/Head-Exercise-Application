Measuring Business Value in Digital Projects

A project can ship on time and still fail to improve the business. Measurement should begin with the problem the investment is expected to solve.

8 November 2026·General educational information

Define the baseline

Record how the process works before change: time per task, error frequency, queue length, repeated entry, support requests or customer wait time. Choose measures linked to the goal and collected consistently. Note seasonal or staffing factors that could affect results.

Combine outcomes with adoption

A login count or page view shows activity, not whether work was completed successfully. Pair adoption measures with task completion, time to resolution, rework, user feedback and reliability. Different stakeholder groups may need different outcome measures.

Review and act on evidence

Set a review date and assign an owner to interpret results. Share what improved, what did not and what cannot yet be concluded. Use the smallest responsible data set that supports a decision rather than collecting detailed personal behaviour unnecessarily.

Key points

  • Establish a baseline before release.
  • Measure successful outcomes as well as activity.
  • Use evidence to decide the next investment.
