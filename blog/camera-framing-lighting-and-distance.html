Camera troubleshooting

Camera Framing, Lighting and Distance Tips

Camera tracking depends on what the browser camera can see. A steady and well-lit view may help the model find facial landmarks, but it cannot guarantee accurate tracking.

8 November 2026·Application help guide

Keep the camera steady

Place the device on a stable surface where the camera does not move during the session. Sudden changes in angle or distance can make the neutral reference less useful. If you reposition the device, stop and recalibrate when the camera session is running.

Use even light

A clear, evenly lit scene can help the tracking model detect facial landmarks. Strong backlighting, deep shadows or rapidly changing light may make detection less reliable. Avoid direct glare and make sure the camera lens is clean.

Keep your face in view

Check that the whole face is visible in the preview and not cut off by the frame. Keep the camera at a practical distance and avoid objects obscuring the face. The app may display a no-face state when the model cannot find a usable face.

Choose comfort over perfect positioning

Do not force a posture or move into an uncomfortable position to satisfy the camera. If the setup does not work for you, use manual mode or stop using the app. The camera is an optional input method, not a requirement.

Key points

  • Use a stable device position.
  • Prefer even, non-glare lighting.
  • Keep the face fully inside the preview.
  • Switch to manual mode if the setup is not comfortable or reliable.
Use and safety note

This guide explains application features, not health treatment or exercise technique. The application is a counting utility, not a medical device or clinical assessment tool. Use only comfortable movement and seek qualified professional advice for health questions.
