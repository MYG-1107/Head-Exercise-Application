Digital Inclusion: Building Web Products for Low-Bandwidth Conditions

A product that works only on a fast connection and a new device excludes people who may need it. Lightweight engineering improves access as well as performance.

8 November 2026·General educational information

Make the core task lightweight

Prioritise essential text and controls before decorative media. Compress images, avoid autoplay video and reduce third-party scripts. A clear static baseline may be more reliable than a complex interface blocked by multiple external services.

Plan for interrupted connections

Save users from repeating long tasks when appropriate. Explain when a change has not synced and provide a retry action. Do not show success until the save operation completes. Set timeouts and handle errors without trapping people in endless loading states.

Test with real constraints

Use responsive layouts, readable text, keyboard support and touch-friendly controls. Test on older devices and throttled networks. Measure page weight over time and remove scripts or features that do not earn their performance and data cost.

Key points

  • Keep the core journey usable before optional assets load.
  • Design clear recovery for interrupted requests.
  • Test on constrained networks and older devices.
