Privacy

Privacy and data handling

No account or login is required. Session values stay in the current page memory, and camera access is optional.

Camera frames

Camera permission is requested only after you select Start camera. The browser provides the camera stream and the application passes frames to the tracking library in the browser. The app does not include a feature that intentionally uploads camera frames, photos or recordings.

External services

Camera mode loads the MediaPipe tracking library and model assets from jsDelivr. The site's Source Sans 3 font is requested from a public font CDN. Those services receive ordinary browser requests and may receive standard connection metadata such as IP address, requested asset URL and browser information. Their own handling of request logs is outside this application's control.

Session counts and exports

Direction counts and elapsed time are held in page memory. They are not saved to an account, cookie, local-storage record or application database. They clear when the page is refreshed or closed. When you select Export summary, your browser downloads a CSV file to your device; the application does not upload that file.

Offline cache

A service worker may cache same-origin application files so the interface and manual mode can load after the first online visit. This cache stores website resources, not camera frames or session counts. Camera mode may need an internet connection to load its external tracking assets.

Your choice

You can use manual mode without camera access. Review browser permissions and clear site data through your browser's settings when needed. Downloaded CSV files are separate files managed by your device.
